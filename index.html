About Us

Dr. Alfredo Pio De Roda Elementary School is dedicated to providing quality education and fostering a nurturing environment for young learners. Our mission is to develop students’ potential and instill in them the values of excellence, integrity, and service.

Vision

A center of excellence for primary education, cultivating learners who are globally competitive, compassionate, and lifelong learners.

Mission

To provide accessible and holistic education that empowers students to reach their fullest potential and contribute meaningfully to society.
