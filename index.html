About Us

Dr. Alfredo Pio De Roda Elementary School is a public institution dedicated to fostering holistic growth and academic excellence among young learners. Our school takes pride in nurturing the values of discipline, integrity, and compassion, preparing students to become responsible citizens and lifelong learners.

Vision & Mission

Vision

A learning community committed to excellence, empowering every learner to reach their full potential in a safe, inclusive, and supportive environment.

Mission

To provide quality basic education through effective teaching, continuous innovation, and active community involvement — ensuring every child develops the knowledge, skills, and values necessary for success in life.
